# Why We Chose the BSD 2-Clause Plus Patent License

Blockchain Commons uses the [BSD 2-Clause Plus Patent License](https://spdx.org/licenses/BSD-2-Clause-Patent.html) to open-source its software. This decision was made after careful consultation with [Kyle Mitchell](https://kemitchell.com), an open-source licensing specialist, member of the [Blue Oak Council](https://blueoakcouncil.org), and author of an excellent [blog](https://writing.kemitchell.com) on these topics. With his input, we selected the BSD license for reasons that are both practical and strategic.

## 1. BSD 2 Is Well-Rated by Experts

The BSD 2-Clause Plus Patent is the **only gold-rated established license** on the [permissive license list](https://blueoakcouncil.org/list) from the Blue Oak Council, a group of open-source licensing experts, of which our own counsel Kyle Mitchell is a member. The license is also [approved by the Open Source Initiative](https://opensource.org/license/bsd-2-clause-patent) as an open-source license, [approved by the Free Software Foundation](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/license-list.html) as GPLv2-compatible, and identified by [SPDX](https://spdx.org/licenses/BSD-2-Clause-Patent.html) as `BSD-2-Clause-Patent`. This makes it a "universal donor" license, compatible with a wide range of other important open-source licenses including GPLv2, something that the commonly used Apache 2.0 license cannot claim.

## 2. BSD 2 Includes Patent Protection

The patent grant component of the BSD 2 license is critical. It ensures that users of this software will never discover after the fact that contributors or stewards have patented features or innovations developed as part of this project and could use those patents against them. We have real concerns that our future problems will not be copyright-related but instead patent-related, particularly whenever large technology companies such as HTC, Apple, and Microsoft are involved, as they have extensive patent portfolios and the resources to pursue them aggressively.

This protection is further reinforced by our membership in the **[Crypto Open Patent Alliance (COPA)](https://www.opencrypto.org)**, a non-profit organization whose members pledge their crypto-related patents into a shared pool, freely available to the entire community. Together, the BSD 2 license and our COPA membership signal our commitment to keeping Blockchain Commons technology permanently open and shielded from patent aggression, both from ourselves and from others.

## 3. BSD 2 is Flexible for Stewardship

Our use of Contributor License Agreements (CLAs) in conjunction with the BSD 2 license enables us to act as effective long-term stewards for our projects. The immediate problem is that each and every large contributor wants to own their copyright, and the big players like HTC, Apple, and Microsoft are the most insistent. Without the copyright assignment of a CLA, this fragments stewardship and makes it very difficult to act decisively in defense of a project or its community.

Having copyright assignment gives us, as stewards, the leverage and flexibility to relicense future releases under a more copyleft regime without having to go back to every contributor. This is not hypothetical: we have already encountered this problem with a derived Shamir library where we needed the original author's permission to move the code to the BSD 2-Clause Plus Patent license. Copyright assignment also gives us the authority to defend against patent challenges from large players, who routinely seek to claim ownership over innovations in open-source projects.

It is worth noting that an open-source license, once released, is a contract that cannot be unilaterally revoked. The history of open source is full of examples where projects have outlived hostile owners, from ZFS to MySQL, precisely because the license itself is irrevocable. The CLA does not change this. What it does is ensure that we, as stewards, retain the authority to act on behalf of the project as a whole, to defend it, to evolve it, and to protect the community that depends on it.

## Conclusion

In summary, the [BSD 2-Clause Plus Patent License](https://spdx.org/licenses/BSD-2-Clause-Patent.html), paired with a CLA and our [COPA](https://www.opencrypto.org) membership, gives users the strongest available protections against patent risk, while giving the project the legal flexibility and authority it needs to survive and evolve over the long term. Where required by the terms of specific financial patrons or grants, we have licensed certain work under other terms but BSD 2-Clause Plus Patent remains our default, and the foundation on which our open-source commitment is built.
